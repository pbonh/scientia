# ADR-0010: Default durable-commitment stages to pause_and_ask

**Status:** proposed

## Y-Statement

In the context of per-stage human-in-loop modes at low-confidence branches,
facing the throughput-vs-safety trade-off, we decided for a per-stage
`autonomous`/`pause_and_ask` policy with `write_design` and `record_adr`
defaulting to `pause_and_ask` (the rest autonomous) and against all-autonomous,
all-pause, or a single global mode, to achieve confidence-gated automation that
is conservative where output is hardest to revise (ASR-4), accepting human
latency at the durable stages. `record_adr` still auto-records at inherited
confidence ≥ 0.90.
