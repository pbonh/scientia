# ADR-0007: Split LLM judgment (skills) from deterministic logic (kg_pipeline)

**Status:** proposed

## Y-Statement

In the context of a pipeline mixing LLM reading with reproducible computation,
facing the need for both judgment and deterministic testable behavior, we
decided for placing judgment in `SKILL.md` and deterministic ops in
`kg_pipeline` (with the validators' error-list as the controller's gate) and
against all-in-skill or all-in-code, to achieve determinism (ASR-2), an
enforceable validation gate (ASR-5), and a small skill budget (ASR-6),
accepting that the LLM controller could skip the gate — addressed for v1 by
skill-eval rubrics. See design Open Question on gate enforceability.
