# ADR-0001: Use the wiki as the sole KG representation

**Status:** accepted

## Y-Statement

In the context of a portable KG-seeded pipeline, facing the need for typed
nodes and kinded edges without a runtime-coupled store, we decided for the
`wiki/` markdown tree as the *only* persistent KG (parsed on demand into
`Page`/`Link` by `kg_pipeline.wiki`) and against a graph DB or vector store, to
achieve portability (ASR-1) and a minimal dependency surface (ASR-9), accepting
O(files) parse cost and no indexed lookups.

**Ratifies shared types:** `kg_pipeline/wiki/__init__.py::Page`,
`kg_pipeline/wiki/__init__.py::Link`.
