# ADR-0002: Encode edge kind in the wiki-link alias slot

**Status:** accepted

## Y-Statement

In the context of a wiki whose edges are markdown links needing a semantic
kind, facing the constraint that edges have no storage outside the body, we
decided for encoding the kind in the alias slot (`[[target | kind]]`, unknown ⇒
`mentions`) and against a frontmatter edge list or bespoke syntax, to achieve
typed provenance at zero extra storage (ASR-7), accepting that the alias slot
is spent on kind rather than display text.
