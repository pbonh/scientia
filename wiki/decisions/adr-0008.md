# ADR-0008: Render templates with str.format_map, no external engine

**Status:** accepted

## Y-Statement

In the context of seven markdown templates rendered by
`kg_pipeline.templates`, facing the templating-mechanism choice under a
minimal-dependency constraint, we decided for `str.format_map` over a flat dict
and against Jinja2, a custom engine, or in-code f-strings, to achieve a minimal
dependency surface (ASR-9) with structure-only templates, accepting no
control flow and brace-escaping.
