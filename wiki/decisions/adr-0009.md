# ADR-0009: Keep all wiki operations non-destructive

**Status:** proposed

## Y-Statement

In the context of `ingest-source` and `audit-wiki` mutating the KG, facing the
risks of false contradictions, stale synthesis, and lost provenance, we decided
for non-destructive operations only (dedupe-in-place, append-only bidirectional
`contradicts` edges leaving older text intact, orphans flagged not deleted) and
against overwrite-on-conflict, auto-resolution, or auto-pruning, to achieve
durable provenance and human adjudication (ASR-8), accepting monotonic growth
and the need for eventual manual pruning.
