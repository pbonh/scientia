## Overview

Simon Brown is the creator of the [[concepts/c4-model|C4 model]] for
visualising software architecture and the author and maintainer of its
official website, [c4model.com](https://c4model.com/). He is an independent
software architecture consultant, speaker, and author who advocates an
**abstraction-first**, developer-friendly approach to documenting and
communicating software architecture.

## Characteristics

- **Role**: Creator of the C4 model; author of the c4model.com reference site.
- **Author**: Has written an O'Reilly-published book on the C4 model, and
  self-published writing on software architecture and documentation.
- **Stance**: Champions notation- and tooling-independent diagramming —
  architecture diagrams as "maps of your code" — and the idea that a small,
  shared set of abstractions matters more than any particular notation
  (UML, ArchiMate, etc.).
- **Community**: Distributes the model under CC BY 4.0 and supports a
  community via training, workshops, video content, Patreon, and Discord.

## Common Strategies

When applying Simon Brown's guidance, teams typically start from the
[[concepts/c4-abstractions|C4 abstractions]] (person, software system,
container, component, code), then draw only the diagram levels that add value —
in practice usually just the **system context** and **container** diagrams —
keeping the notation simple and the diagrams maintainable. His representative
contribution is reframing architecture documentation as zoomable maps rather
than a single exhaustive diagram.
